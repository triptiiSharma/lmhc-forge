## Why this exists (task §15's non-circularity requirement)

`checker_verdict` cannot be evaluated for accuracy against itself, and
`source_intent_label` (which construct was *requested*) is not
automatically the binary truth — compilation can silently change or remove
the requested structure. `binary_ground_truth` must come from an
independent process: two humans reading raw SASS, blind to what the
checker said and blind to which construct/context/architecture they're
looking at, reconciling disagreements.

## Primary sample is core-matrix-only

The primary sample is drawn **exclusively** from the 2,016-cell core
matrix. The 2 pilot flag-recheck artifacts (`Z1×K0` and `Z2×K3` with
`-maxrregcount=16 --use_fast_math`) are **not** part of the core matrix,
are tagged `SUPPLEMENTAL_PILOT` in the private mapping, are included in the
review packet for completeness, but are excluded from the primary
core-sample accuracy calculation and reported separately in Phase 6.

## What was sanitized, and how it was verified (not just asserted)

Every sampled artifact's real, compiled SASS (not synthetic, not
re-generated) had exactly 4 lines redacted per file (verified exhaustive
by scanning every line of every one of the 254 original files for any
architecture-identifying token before writing the sanitizer, not assumed
from a handful of examples — full account in `PHASE5_BLINDING_AUDIT.md`,
including the real bug this process found and fixed, a `.target sm_NN`
line the first pass missed):
1. `code for smXX` → `code for [ARCH-REDACTED]`
2. `.target smXX` → `.target [ARCH-REDACTED]` (present in ~half the files)
3. `Function : <mangled-name>` → `Function : R####`
4. `.headerflags @"...EF_CUDA_SMXX..."` → SM references redacted

Every instruction line — opcode, predicate, register, address, hex
encoding — is copied byte-for-byte. A SHA-256 of both the original and
sanitized text is recorded in the private mapping so the correspondence
between what a reviewer saw and the real compiled artifact is always
independently re-verifiable later.

## Blinded review protocol

Unchanged from the original design (still correct):

1. Each reviewer works only from their own `phase5_review/reviewer_{A,B}/`
   directory — their CSV plus their `sass/` folder — never from the
   private mapping, never from the other reviewer's files, and never by
   running `classify_binary.py` on these artifacts until their own label
   is recorded.
2. `PHASE5_REVIEW_CODEBOOK.md` is given to both reviewers verbatim,
   defining all 5 categories with neutral examples, given to both
   identically so disagreements reflect genuine judgment calls.
3. Independent labeling, private copies, no visibility into the other
   reviewer's answers until both are complete.
4. Disagreements (`reviewer_1_label != reviewer_2_label`) are resolved by
   joint re-examination into a `resolved_binary_ground_truth`.
5. `results/phase5_private_mapping.csv` is consulted only **after** all
   254 rows have a resolved ground truth — that comparison is Phase 6,
   not Phase 5.

## Phase 6 evaluation policy — frozen now, before any human label exists

Pre-registered so the acceptance policy and metric set cannot be tuned
after seeing results. **Not computed until both human reviews are
complete.**

**Primary acceptance policy** (binary, security-oriented):
- `FULL_SIGNATURE` → checker **acceptance**.
- `PARTIAL_SIGNATURE`, `STORE_ONLY`, `ABSENT` → checker **rejection**.
- `UNDECIDABLE` → **abstention** (excluded from the accept/reject
  confusion matrix, reported separately as an abstention rate).

**Metrics to compute, once ground truth exists:**
- raw inter-reviewer agreement (% of 254 rows where `reviewer_1_label ==
  reviewer_2_label`)
- Cohen's kappa between the two reviewers
- number and distribution (by category pair) of disagreements
- exact multiclass accuracy of `checker_verdict` vs.
  `resolved_binary_ground_truth`, after disagreement resolution
- per-class precision and recall (5×5, all categories)
- full 5×5 confusion matrix (`checker_verdict` × `resolved_binary_ground_truth`)
- false accepts (ground truth is not `FULL_SIGNATURE`, checker said
  `FULL_SIGNATURE`) and false rejects (ground truth is `FULL_SIGNATURE`,
  checker said otherwise), under the frozen acceptance policy above
- abstention/`UNDECIDABLE` rate, both the checker's and the reviewers'
- security-oriented false-accept rate specifically (false accepts ÷ all
  ground-truth non-`FULL_SIGNATURE` cases) — the number that matters most
  for any claim about the checker being trustworthy as a gate
- all of the above computed **for the 252-artifact core sample only**;
  the 2 `SUPPLEMENTAL_PILOT` artifacts' results reported as a separate,
  clearly labeled 2-row addendum, never pooled into the core numbers
